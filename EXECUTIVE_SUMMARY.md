**Date:** January 13, 2026

## The Problem

Literature review found **critical issues** with current clinical presets:
- ❌ Working memory contradiction (citing conflicting sources)
- ❌ 3 unverifiable citations
- ❌ Many parameters are theoretical estimates, not empirical
- ❌ No documentation of scale mappings
- ❌ Over-claiming "empirically grounded"

**Risk:** Reviewers will identify these issues and reject publication.

---

## The Solution: Four-Phase Plan

### **Phase 1: Critical Fixes** (2 weeks)
**Fix blockers immediately**
- Resolve WM contradiction → use Cowan (4±1)
- Verify/replace 3 problematic citations
- Add confidence levels (HIGH/MODERATE/LOW)
- Add honest disclaimers

**Output:** Defensible for research use

---

### **Phase 2: Evidence Strengthening** (6 weeks)
**Fill literature gaps**
- Systematic search for 20+ new papers
- Extract effect sizes from meta-analyses
- Document all scale mappings
- Expert consultation (4 domain specialists)

**Output:** Comprehensive evidence base

---

### **Phase 3: Validation & Testing** (6 weeks)
**Empirical validation**
- Sensitivity analysis (identify critical parameters)
- Simulation-to-data validation (fit to empirical datasets)
- Cross-validation (generalization testing)
- Refine parameters based on results

**Output:** Empirically validated model

---

### **Phase 4: Documentation & Release** (2 weeks)
**Professional publication**
- Update all documentation
- Write methods paper
- Create data/code archive
- Submit to peer-reviewed journal

**Output:** Publication-ready v1.2
