# Clinical Presets

This document provides an overview of the clinical presets available in RPM-EE.

## Presets

The following clinical presets are available:

-   **NT (Neurotypical)**: The baseline preset, representing a typical individual.
-   **ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder)**: Characterized by increased attentional variability and impulsivity.
-   **ASD (Autism Spectrum Disorder)**: Characterized by differences in social communication and interaction, and restricted, repetitive patterns of behavior, interests, or activities.
-   **MDD (Major Depressive Disorder)**: Characterized by persistent low mood and loss of interest or pleasure.
