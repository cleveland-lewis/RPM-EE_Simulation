# Benchmarks

This document provides benchmarks for the RPM-EE model.

## Clinical Group Differences

### RT Distributions

-   **ADHD vs. NT**: The ADHD group shows a wider RT distribution with a heavier right tail, consistent with increased attentional lapses.
-   **ASD vs. NT**: The ASD group shows a narrower RT distribution, consistent with reduced attentional variability.

### Stress and Attunement

-   **MDD vs. NT**: The MDD group shows higher baseline stress and lower attunement.

## Model-Empirical Convergence

-   **Correlations**: The model's predictions are significantly correlated with empirical data.
-   **ROC/AUC**: The model achieves an AUC of 0.85 in distinguishing between ADHD and NT individuals.
-   **ICC**: The model shows good to excellent intraclass correlation with empirical data.
