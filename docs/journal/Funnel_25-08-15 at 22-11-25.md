- 10:11 PM: Replay
- This is ability to take memories and repeat them. 
- Replay will allow for semanticization of episodic memories that have achieved target salience made clear by salience tagging processes. 
- Depending on the recurrence of information relevant to the memory of concern to be replayed and its salience and the individual in question, the memories may be replayed persistently. 
- Replays can provide new realizations and insights. 
- Depending on memory decay, which is dependent on the salience and occurrence or relevant and potentially novel stimuli, memories that have been replayed to the point of tire or satisfaction of any sort, they may sit in wait for novel and relevant information.   
- Novel and relevant information to the memories in store can trigger a resurgence of analysis involving the addition of the new information. 
- Without novel and relevant information the memories may continue to decay to the point of complete decay, though this depends on the individuals’ memory capabilities.  [[quick-capture]]
